What we do

A detail with a purpose.

We detail vehicles.

Hand wash, wax and protect, streak-free glass, steam clean interior, carpet shampoo, done right. We come to you, anywhere in San Diego. Book anytime.

100% to underprivileged golfers.

Every dollar of profit is donated to Pro Kids, First Tee, San Diego, the nonprofit that provides clubs, bags, coaching, and programs to kids in City Heights and Oceanside who can't afford to play. We share every receipt.

First responders & military free.

Firefighters, EMTs, police, active-duty and veterans, anyone who serves, the detail is on us. Show a badge or military ID, leave with a clean vehicle. No questions.

Our story

Local roots. Lasting purpose.

Sudzy Studz started the summer before our sophomore year with James, Brody, and a pressure washer. We knocked on doors, learned how to price jobs, and built it into a real operation with a little under $20,000 in revenue over two years.

In summer 2026, we formalized as an LLC and changed the model: 100% of profits now go to Pro Kids, First Tee, San Diego to help kids access golf equipment, coaching, and mentorship they might not otherwise have.

We have been fortunate to have that access ourselves, so putting this business toward kids who do not was the only move that made sense. First responders and military still get details on us, always.
